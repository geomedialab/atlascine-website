Dr. Sepideh Shahamati will be presenting her work on mapping evictions in Parc-Extension (Montreal) - including the [Atlas of Intangible Heritage](https://rs-atlascine.concordia.ca/parcextension/index.html) she has developed with Atlascine - at three events in this month :

**April 16**: [CBAR Symposium](https://cbarparcex.ca/projects/the-first-symposium-of-cbar-premier-colloque-de-la-recherche-action-du-cbar/) - *Action-Research Dialogue in Parc-Extension: A Neighbourhood Under Pressure Yet United*, Brique par Brique, 1:00 - 4:00 PM

**April 22**: [Voices for Housing Justice Zine Launch](https://www.concordia.ca/cuevents/artsci/social-justice-centre/2026/04/22/housing-justice-zine.html), SHIFT Centre for Social Transformation, Concordia University, 5:00 - 7:00 PM

**April 30**: [Radio Renoviction Radio Show](https://ckut.ca/playlists/RN), CKUT (90.3 FM), 5:00 - 6:00 PM
