---
title: "Workflow for text data in Atlascine"
date: '2026-01-23'
---

📖 📚 🔖
Following increasing interest, we have added a [page to the wiki](https://github.com/geomedialab/atlascine/wiki/Working-with-text-media) which provides instructions for working with text-based media (such as novels or interview transcripts) in Atlascine, without an accompanying media (audio or video) file. Although at the moment it is more of a workaround and requires some manual processing, we are working on simplifying this process and perhaps eventually integrating native support for text-based media in Atlascine.
